I've been working through my thecatamites/garmentdistrict backlog and got hooked on the Anthology of The Killer series over the past few days. The collection of nine short games was finally released for the Switch and PS5 a few months ago. Really deep art design, soundtrack, and world building! I don't really like horror games but these aren't really scary--just a bit gory. It's horror/comedy.

  • https://thecatamites.itch.io/anthology-of-the-killer
  • Voice of the Killer (Episode 1 no commentary lets play)

They are basically walking simulators so not much play--but each game reveals its plot in a different way which is pretty interesting. It also presents minor* philosophical questions buried a little deep within abstraction... good material for the subconscious to munch on for a few days...

* well perhaps a bit more serious than "is a hot dog a sandwich??"; maybe you could say lowercase m-major or lowercase p-profound philosophical questions
