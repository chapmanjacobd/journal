Logitech M575S

I can't speak for the S version but the M575 works perfectly okay for at least 18 months and then it poops out. If you like the trackball placement it's not a terrible option just one that you might have to replace every couple years or so.

However, if you prefer Trackman Marble style I can heartily recommend the Nulea M505. The battery lasts forever between charges and it has shown no signs of degradation so far. I have a feeling it will last a bit longer as it likely has less planned obsolescence.
