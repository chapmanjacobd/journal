We already know what a world without work looks like - Asterisk Magazine

Basically, drawing parallels to 18th-century European aristocrats, the author argues that a future post-work world driven by automation will revert humanity into a highly personal, status-obsessed "relational society" centered around gossip and dominance hierarchies.

In contrast, modern work and impersonal market economies foster liberal individualism, allowing people to derive dignity from objective accomplishments and freeing personal relationships from transactional necessity.

Losing modern work to AI risks eroding these individualist values and returning society to a more clannish, restrictive way of life. But maybe that's already happening anyway? Or maybe elites have never ever actually been defined by their job roles except as brief periods of cosplay?
