There are a couple different versions of Maniac Mansion... I haven't played the original but I've played Maniac Mansion Deluxe and can recommend playing it that way. It appears to be the same game but with updated graphics.

But if you're going for the full 8-bit nostalgia factor--all official releases of Day of the Tentacle contain the complete, fully playable original Maniac Mansion as a built-in secret "mini-game"!

5 Days a Stranger is the first game in the Chzo Mythos series. If you enjoy it definitely play 7 Days a Skeptic, Trilby's Notes, 6 Days a Sacrifice, and Trilby: Art of Theft.
