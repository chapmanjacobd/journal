I think there's some aspect of https://xkcd.com/915/ going on. I didn't like action films for a long time but recently I've been watching more and you begin to appreciate certain aspects of them.

What I appreciate about action scenes now is a sense of choreography and a meditation on the emotions and feelings of the different characters in the scene.

A good action scene in my mind helps the viewer to feel more integrated into the character on screen. Bad action scenes make you disbelieve the film more and bring you out of the story.
