If you're in Osaka 大阪王将 なんば千日前店 is pretty cheap and tasty (my wife and I shared a meal for like $7 total)! For a more luxury (but still budget) option you could get some Kaiseki like 京料理 御旅町田ごと.

Lots of South Korea smaller cities (like Gyeongju and smaller) are also very lovely to travel to and often quite a bit cheaper than Japan but have a similar feeling and very good food! It's nice to get some contrast every once in a while. Palate cleanser?

It's often just a small marginal cost to add another city to a trip. Is this a bit like suggesting a European to add Mexico City to their USA trip? Maybe, but I suppose I would also encourage that because CDMX is pretty neat too if you know the right neighborhoods!
