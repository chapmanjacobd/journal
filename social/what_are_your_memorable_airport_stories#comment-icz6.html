I remember being an unaccompanied minor in the ATL airport for a layover, being really hungry, and the line at the pulled pork sandwich shop was really tiny (designed for people not luggage) and it was already crowded full of people.

I guess my parents were too cheap to pay for checked luggage because I had a big suitcase that was almost as big as me that I was using as a carry-on. So I asked a security lady if she could watch my suitcase while I ordered food, and of course she said no.

I was surprised by this because I didn't know yet about the dangers of unattended luggage so I thought she was just being mean. I thought security people were supposed to look after bags and prevent thieves, right? But instead they exist for a more abstract purpose. And so I somehow ordered food but because I had a big suitcase taking up the whole entrance I moved out and waited to the side of the restaurant but I was too far away to hear them call my order number.

So I waited to the side of the restaurant awkwardly for 20 minutes and it seemed like everyone was getting their food fast so I was surprised that my order was taking so long and when I went back to ask about my order number I guess it was already completed about 20 minutes ago because it was already kinda cold.
