Installed a Della Optima Series 12k BTU 230v SEER2 23 Ultra Heat (GREE rebadge) mini-split just in time for the recent barrage of summer-time heatwaves. It has actually been keeping up pretty well, including removing humidity from the air.

Although, it seems like even though I set it to 24c I have to set the fan to turbo mode every once in a while or it won't read that the other side of the room is already 26c.

Google tells me it feels like 45c outside for the next two days but the actual temperature will only briefly peak at 36c around 5pm. By Friday the weather in western Illinois will be back to a normal but humid 21~24c.

I have a little Xiaomi Smart Temperature and Humidity Monitor 3 Mini which has a cute smile or frown if the temperature or humidity gets too crazy. But sometimes it will be 26c and feel cold while othertimes it is 23c and feels to hot even while humidity is around 40% both times. So I think humans don't prefer one stable temperature.
