If you want to splurge a little it's definitely worth it to get MicroLED, RGB MiniLED, or QD MiniLED but the marketing jargon is often confusing where it's not 100% clear what you're getting compared to words like CRT and OLED. So it can help to compare different models carefully. Subscribing to rtings for a month can definitely be worth it.

Almost every modern smart TV operating system incorporates ads and sponsored/targeted content into the home menu. It's really hard to get away from that--nay impossible--without just keeping the smart TV offline.

Some brands are more aggressive at this than others... for their software I'd avoid Amazon Fire TVs, Insignia, Samsung, LG, Vizio.

There's also the privacy issue: LG Isn't the Only TV Company Spying on You

Sony, TCL, and Hisense are competent brands and their software usually isn't too terrible... but the overall experience can vary a lot by model.

My Hisense TV is probably spying on me but I just keep it offline and in apps-only mode so the interface is very simple
