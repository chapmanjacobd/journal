I was surprised how violent Alone in the Dark was! Various aspects remind me of Resident Evil like the fixed camera locations like watching a movie, tank controls... The graphics aged a bit poorly but several parts creeped me out.

I didn't know much about the history of either games but looking into it more Shinji Mikami has acknowledged that Alone in the Dark was the primary inspiration that shaped the development of Resident Evil. So I'm not making any novel comparisons here.
