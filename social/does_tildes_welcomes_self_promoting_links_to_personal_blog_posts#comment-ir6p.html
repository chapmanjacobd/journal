In the past six months you've posted your blog about every week or so. Is this a bit too much? Maybe a bit. Are you cross posting every blog article that you write to Tildes? Are you doing it because you want some link juice, or you want agreement, or you want criticism, or you want a discussion about the topic?

Maybe it is some combination of two or three of those. I don't want to discourage you from posting on Tildes but maybe you could post 3rd party links a bit more? If it's your blog then you have a bit more weight in the discussion (as your opinion is the topic) which isn't always fair... but sometimes it is right to post your blog instead of a comment. I don't think we should ban your blog--it's easy to ignore if we don't want to read it this week.

Maybe percentage is not an issue for people but frequency is?--If you linked your site every day I think people would appreciate it less. If you linked to your site once or twice per month people would still know your blog exists. I don't think you post too often but it does seem a bit of a borderline case.

When judging others it helps me to think about myself doing the same action. Personally, I'd be a bit scared to post my own blog post to Tildes. Maybe I lack confidence or don't feel I could put something coherent together which would be interesting for a broad number of people to read. The french have a word: egoist. On the other hand... if everyone is scared to share then all we see on Tildes are virtual tumbleweeds (which Deimos would need to program in--as a new feature!--to maintain the same level of user retention). So some vanity is useful in the world and it is not all bad. I like reading your blog every now and then! But the topic of your writing is not always interesting so I don't always read it--and that is perfectly fine.
